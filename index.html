About the Project

Million Star Resort is an eco-luxury desert retreat in Iran. Each private villa features a glass-ceiling bedroom for stargazing and a secluded pool—crafted for digital detox, serenity, and unforgettable stories.

Our Purpose

Our Vision

To redefine hospitality by fusing stargazing architecture, sustainable design, and tokenized reservations—transparent, transferable, and global.

How It Works

Purchase during the presale and our smart contract automatically sends tokens to your wallet. Each token represents one night you can stay at Million Star Resort.

Tokens are transferable on Polygon. You can hold, use to book nights, or resell them on secondary markets. Fractional ownership grants proportional discount for a single night.
